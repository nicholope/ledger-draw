## 4. VARIANCE ANALYSIS

### Hard Costs — MEP/HVAC ($112,500.00)
Mechanical, electrical, and plumbing systems represent a major line item in construction. The HVAC invoice of $112,500.00 reflects the first significant draw on MEP work and is within expected project scope. This falls well within the allocated $350,000.00 budget for this category. **No action required.**

### Hard Costs — Landscaping ($68,300.00 — Invoices #1016 and #1017)
Landscaping costs are split across two invoices totaling $68,300.00, representing staged work completed in mid-April and early May. The split invoicing is normal for landscaping contractors and reflects phased site completion. Budget allocation of $420,000.00 indicates this is approximately 16% of planned landscaping spend. **No action required.**

### Soft Costs — Project Management Fee ($28,800.00)
The monthly project management fee of $28,800.00 is consistent with the established monthly draw rate. Year-to-date soft costs ($363,200.00) have exceeded the initial soft costs budget of $350,000.00 by $13,200.00, likely reflecting front-loaded architecture and consulting fees. This variance is acceptable given project complexity. **Recommend monitoring going forward.**

### Change Order #002 — Contingency Draw ($14,200.00)
Change Order #002 drawn against contingency represents field modifications totaling $14,200.00. Year-to-date contingency usage is $37,000.00 of the $80,000.00 allocated budget, leaving $28,800.00 available. **[DOCUMENTATION REQUIRED] — Signed Change Order #002 and supporting scope/cost justification must accompany this draw.**
