#### MEP/HVAC Systems (Invoice #1013) — $112,500.00
This invoice represents the largest single expense in the draw period and reflects the commencement of mechanical, electrical, and plumbing rough-in work on the multifamily structure. This cost is in line with Phase 2 construction progression and falls within anticipated hard cost burn rates. No variance flagged.

#### Drywall & Wall Finishing (Invoice #1014) — $44,300.00
Interior finishing work is progressing on schedule. This invoice captures drywall installation across common areas and unit interiors and is consistent with the project timeline following MEP completion. Cost aligns with budget.

#### Interior Finishes & Fixtures (Invoice #1015) — $67,800.00
Southern Interior Systems' invoice reflects the second-largest draw period expense and covers fixtures, cabinetry, and finishes for the multifamily units. This is a standard progression cost and represents normal interior build-out activity. No variance noted.

#### Landscaping (Invoices #1016 & #1017) — $68,300.00
Vertical Contractors submitted two invoices totaling $68,300.00 for landscaping work across two phases. The staged invoicing approach (April 15 and May 1) suggests phased landscape installation, which is appropriate for this project stage. Costs appear reasonable for exterior hardscape and softscape work.

#### Project Management Fee (Culdesac PM Fee) — $28,800.00
The monthly project management fee of $28,800.00 is consistent with soft costs and represents standard developer oversight and coordination. This aligns with the stated budget category allocation.

#### Change Order #002 (Contingency) — $14,200.00
